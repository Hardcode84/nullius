Version: 0.0.7
Date: 10.11.2026
  Features:
    - Added early rocket launches, lighter titanium rocket stages, and new rocket fuels, including aluminum fuel on Vulcanus.
    - Added space platforms for carrying cargo between planets, and pneumatic rocket silos on Vulcanus.
    - Vulcanus and Fulgora are now reached through probe research.
    - Added radio astronomy: record signals on each planet and combine them into astronomy research.
    - Added copper production on Fulgora through deep drilling, and helium from Fulgora hydrocarbons.
    - Reworked nuclear power: uranium now comes from seawater and is enriched in gas centrifuges, and reactors are separate research from fusion fuel.
    - Antimatter is now collected in Fulgora orbit, and filled traps must be recharged before they spoil.
    - Added an admin command for free personal logistics while testing.
  Changes:
    - Removed asteroid mining; the copper and uranium mission goals no longer apply.
    - Terraforming, paving, excavation, seeding, and carbon sequestration drones now work only on Nauvis.
    - Removed two nuclear checkpoints that unlocked nothing.
    - The mining checkpoint now accepts crushed ores and sand.
    - Rebalanced rocket cargo weights.
    - Added missing research descriptions and effects.
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed recipes that unlocked before their ingredients could be made.
    - Fixed ore checkpoints counting crushed ore twice.
    - Fixed missing fluorine and hydrogen fluoride barrels.
    - Fixed infinite mining productivity being available before its earlier levels.
    - Fixed small and large assemblers being drawn slightly off-centre.
  Compatibility:
    - Loading older saves removes asteroid miners, guide drones, and their remotes; asteroid ore patches already on the map remain.
    - Loading older saves moves antimatter traps out of reactors and vehicles onto the ground.

Version: 0.0.2
Date: 13.09.2026
  Features:
    - Added water quenching for iron and aluminum bloom casting. Water increases plate and rod yields at the same bloom consumption rate.
    - Added Vulcanus tier-2 geology and climatology science recipes, including boxed bulk recipes, using locally processed ingredients.
  Changes:
    - Probe research now places an idle android on Vulcanus. Any player on the same force can use it, including players who join later.
    - Players can use a shared probe body after another player leaves it. Occupied bodies cannot be taken over.
    - The /nullius-vulcanus command switches the caller to the probe body if it is idle and reports when another player occupies it.
    - Volcanic gas analysis now accepts compressed volcanic gas.
  Bugfixes:
    - Added local residual-gas separation so Vulcanus can produce argon before physics research.
    - Fixed body selection and map markers after body changes, respawn, and player removal in multiplayer.
    - Fixed an error when a force is created before the first player joins.
