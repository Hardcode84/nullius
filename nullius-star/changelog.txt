Version: 0.0.2
Date: 13.09.2026
  Features:
    - Added water quenching for iron and aluminum bloom casting. Water increases plate and rod yields at the same bloom consumption rate.
    - Added Vulcanus tier-2 geology and climatology science recipes, including boxed bulk recipes, using locally processed ingredients.
  Changes:
    - Probe research now places an idle android on Vulcanus. Any player on the same force can use it, including players who join later.
    - Players can use a shared probe body after another player leaves it. Occupied bodies cannot be taken over.
    - The /nullius-vulcanus command switches the caller to the probe body if it is idle and reports when another player occupies it.
    - Volcanic gas analysis now accepts compressed volcanic gas.
  Bugfixes:
    - Added local residual-gas separation so Vulcanus can produce argon before physics research.
    - Fixed body selection and map markers after body changes, respawn, and player removal in multiplayer.
    - Fixed an error when a force is created before the first player joins.
