# Processed-input Vulcanus science versus Nauvis with second-tier miners

Generated by `tools/compare_factorio_factory_plans.py`.

This is a hypothetical recipe experiment. No gameplay recipes are changed. All eligible supply routes use the declared pre-physics machine catalog. This does not prove ingredient availability at the initial science tier 2 unlock.

Geology candidate: 1 glass, 1 lime and 2 mineral dust produce 2 packs in 10 seconds. The boxed recipe scales material and output quantities by five and takes 50 seconds.

Climatology candidate: 100 compressed carbon dioxide, 10 compressed nitrogen and 5 sulfuric acid produce 2 packs in 10 seconds. Its boxed recipe scales quantities and time by five.

Nauvis is restricted to geology 2 and climatology 2, including their boxed equivalents. Vulcanus is restricted to the candidate recipes. Both support factories use all eligible alternative recipes.

Combined means each science type is produced at the stated rate. Independent chains are separate factories. Compare the combined row to measure shared co-products.

Counts include process machines and extraction. Nauvis receives external electricity; generation and distribution are excluded. Vulcanus fuel production is included. Both exclude transport, storage and heat delivery. Electric MW is average load plus installed drain.

The solver minimizes active machine time. It does not minimize rounded station counts or construction costs. Construction flows must be feasible. Native machine productivity is included; research productivity, modules and beacons are excluded.

Nauvis uses small miner 2 for solid resources. Gas extraction uses extractor 1. This separates mining capacity from recipe balance.

## Declared boundaries

### Nauvis with miner 2

- **comparison:** Same post-pneumatic research entrance as the Vulcanus comparison. Comparable first/second-tier processing machines. All pre-physics recipes are eligible. No modules or beacons. Guaranteed outputs only.
- **construction:** All process placement items are produced. Continuous fractional construction flow is not a startup schedule. Rutile and graphite seed stock is declared separately.
- **electricity:** External continuous electric grid. Report active demand and drain for rounded installed stations. Generation, storage and power distribution are not counted.
- **experiment:** Independent and combined tier 2 science capacity. All pre-physics recipes are eligible for supply and construction. Ordinary and boxed tier 2 routes are included. No labs, modules, beacons or technology productivity bonus. Construction has no free placement stock.
- **recipe_boundary:** Only ordinary Nauvis tier 2 science recipes and their boxed equivalents are eligible.
- **resources:** Iron ore, bauxite, sandstone, limestone patches and a fumarole at 100 percent yield. Mining machines are counted. No ore imports. No mining productivity bonus. This sensitivity uses small miner 2 instead of small miner 1; gas extraction is unchanged.
- **time:** All science lines and labs are available at time zero. Research triggers are assumed satisfied. Times exclude construction, checkpoints and exploration.
- **waste:** Only listed solid waste is removed. Fluid disposal uses real recipes.

### Vulcanus candidate

- **construction:** Compute placement-item demand beyond wreck stock and its production flow. Supplied machine catalog is not a timed startup sequence.
- **experiment:** Independent and combined tier 2 science capacity. All pre-physics recipes are eligible for supply and construction. Ordinary and boxed tier 2 routes are included. No labs, modules, beacons or technology productivity bonus. Construction has no free placement stock.
- **heat:** Conserve heat using resolved heat interfaces and runtime controller constants, at continuous duty with ideal delivery. Report temperature-class demands; geometry, sampled-duty aliasing and warmup require a runtime layout witness.
- **labs:** Size speed-1 pneumatic labs for the fastest supplied research cycle. Reserve full-load lab fuel in the flow; do not credit lab waste heat.
- **objective:** Minimize active process-machine seconds with all eligible alternative recipes and co-products.
- **productivity:** Native machine effects included. No modules, beacons, or technology productivity bonus.
- **rates:** Configured industrial comparison points, not an assertion of optimal player targets.
- **raw:** Local HCl extraction at declared 100 percent geyser yield. Extractor count and fuel are included; site availability and depletion are not a supplied map witness.
- **recipe_boundary:** Only the declared candidate final science recipes are eligible. Candidates are hypothetical and assumed unlocked; research timing is not modeled.
- **solid_waste:** Only listed solid residues may leave the model. Surplus rates are reported. Filled barrels cannot serve as fluid waste sinks. Fluid waste requires a real recipe.
- **standalone:** Starter and industrial single-pack rows are independent full supply networks without labs; shared factory co-products can change these costs. Industrial rows allow all pre-physics recipes and the declared second-tier machine catalog.
- **time:** Base research supply bound at the configured simultaneous pack rates, not human construction time. Checkpoints are separate.
- **uncertainty:** Guaranteed outputs only, never expected probabilistic returns.
