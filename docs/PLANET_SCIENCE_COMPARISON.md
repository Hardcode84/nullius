# Nauvis and Vulcanus physics science capacity

Generated by `tools/compare_factorio_factory_plans.py`.

Both plans start after the same pneumatic technology prerequisite closure. This is a matched remaining-research comparison, not a new-game Nauvis schedule.

Nauvis supplies six science types at the stated rate, including physics. Vulcanus also supplies its local metallurgic science at that rate.

Each single-pack stage is an independent supply chain without labs. Do not add these station counts to the full factory.

Nauvis includes ore miners and gas extraction, but receives electricity from an external grid. Its station count excludes generation, storage and power distribution. Vulcanus includes its fluid-fuel production network; process heat demand is internal heat delivery, not extra fuel demand.

Both plans exclude transport, storage and a placed factory layout. No modules, beacons or mining productivity bonuses are applied. Each recipe has separate rounded stations. Rates assume continuous duty.

Nauvis climatology uses natural volcanic gas, nitrogen from air separation, and wastewater from seawater filtration. Geology uses boxed recipes and obtains crushed iron from bauxite disposal. These are complete independent chains; a shared factory can use different co-products.

Equal science rates give almost equal research supply times. The main difference is the production network and its energy supply. Scheduled times assume all lines and labs are available at time zero and all triggers are satisfied. They do not measure player completion time.

Electric MW is continuous average demand plus installed drain. It is not a measured peak load. The planner minimizes active station time, not electricity use or total construction cost.

## Declared boundaries

### Nauvis

- **comparison:** Same post-pneumatic research entrance as the Vulcanus comparison. Comparable first/second-tier processing machines. All pre-physics recipes are eligible. No modules or beacons. Guaranteed outputs only.
- **construction:** All process placement items are produced. Continuous fractional construction flow is not a startup schedule. Rutile and graphite seed stock is declared separately.
- **electricity:** External continuous electric grid. Report active demand and drain for rounded installed stations. Generation, storage and power distribution are not counted.
- **resources:** Iron ore, bauxite, sandstone, limestone patches and a fumarole at 100 percent yield. Mining machines are counted. No ore imports. No mining productivity bonus.
- **time:** All science lines and labs are available at time zero. Research triggers are assumed satisfied. Times exclude construction, checkpoints and exploration.
- **waste:** Only listed solid waste is removed. Fluid disposal uses real recipes.

### Vulcanus

- **construction:** Compute placement-item demand beyond wreck stock and its production flow. Supplied machine catalog is not a timed startup sequence.
- **heat:** Conserve heat using resolved heat interfaces and runtime controller constants, at continuous duty with ideal delivery. Report temperature-class demands; geometry, sampled-duty aliasing and warmup require a runtime layout witness.
- **labs:** Size speed-1 pneumatic labs for the fastest supplied research cycle. Reserve full-load lab fuel in the flow; do not credit lab waste heat.
- **objective:** Minimize active process-machine seconds with all eligible alternative recipes and co-products.
- **productivity:** Native machine effects included. No modules, beacons, or technology productivity bonus.
- **rates:** Configured industrial comparison points, not an assertion of optimal player targets.
- **raw:** Local HCl extraction at declared 100 percent geyser yield. Extractor count and fuel are included; site availability and depletion are not a supplied map witness.
- **solid_waste:** Only listed solid residues may leave the model. Surplus rates are reported. Filled barrels cannot serve as fluid waste sinks. Fluid waste requires a real recipe.
- **standalone:** Starter and industrial single-pack rows are independent full supply networks without labs; shared factory co-products can change these costs. Industrial rows allow all pre-physics recipes and the declared second-tier machine catalog.
- **time:** Base research supply bound at the configured simultaneous pack rates, not human construction time. Checkpoints are separate.
- **uncertainty:** Guaranteed outputs only, never expected probabilistic returns.
